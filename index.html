Why Technoblade?

His Minecraft skills were impressive, but his humour was just as much a part of his videos. He could turn something as ordinary as farming potatoes into a whole rivalry.

This is a small fan tribute to his videos and the community around them.

Dream vs Technoblade

The Dream vs Technoblade duel deserves a place on this page. Two great players, a close match, and Techno taking the win, 6–4. It’s a moment worth coming back to.

His own analysis is worth watching too: he breaks down the fight in his usual funny, matter-of-fact way.
